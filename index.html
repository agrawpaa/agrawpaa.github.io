Rough Heston Volatility Surface CalibrationIndependent research, Fall 2026

Teaching a neural network to fit a rough-volatility surface

I built a PyTorch engine that calibrates the Rough Heston model (H, ν, θ, ρ, V0) to option surfaces. A physics-informed network is trained against a Markovian-projection PDE residual, with derivatives from autograd.

A hybrid-scheme Monte Carlo simulator generates synthetic option chains with known ground-truth parameters, so I can check whether calibration recovers them. The slider at the top of this page shows why H matters: lower H makes short-dated skew much steeper.

Calendar, butterfly, and static-bound no-arbitrage penalties keep fitted surfaces admissible. A validation suite reports implied-vol error, parameter recovery, and arbitrage diagnostics.

Shadow Credit and ABS Pricing EnginePurdue Momentum, Finance Pillar
Project manager, Aug 2025 to May 2026

How hidden Buy Now, Pay Later debt could underprice consumer ABS

I led a 6-person team studying how unrecorded BNPL debt drives loan stacking. Automated Python and SQL pipelines pulled delinquency indicators from FRED, CFPB complaints, and SEC EDGAR filings.

An OLS model in statsmodels put the default multiplier at 1.2x to 2.0x under severe debt-stacking stress. I then built a 48-month Senior, Mezzanine, and Equity waterfall with CPR/SMM prepayment vectors and sequential loss allocation.

Debt shocks breached subordination and impaired principal on the BBB-rated tranche. The result was a long AAA, short BBB trade thesis, delivered as a credit memo and pitch deck.
